# Cultural identity via path motifs, not adire textile

The game's primary West-African cultural fingerprint is the **orisha's natural force** — a
per-path atmospheric re-theme of the main screen (Sango storm, Osun river, Ane earth) — rather
than the **adire textile geometry** that `ART_BIBLE.md` §1 foregrounds as "what keeps it from
reading as generic fantasy." Before a path is chosen, a neutral *ascent signature* (a rising
Àṣẹ thread into a constellation, plus the proverb) carries the identity.

## Why

- In-engine, **motion + palette + a natural-force motif** read as distinct, premium, and
  unmistakably specific: storm vs river vs earth are obvious at a glance.
- A ghosted adire cloth texture risked reading as **generic or muddy** at phone scale, and the
  textile is harder to animate and re-theme than a tinted, particle-driven ambient.
- It leans into the per-path accent palettes the Art Bible already defines (§1).

## Considered and rejected

- **Assertive adire on key surfaces** (eléko-grid frames, oníko rings, woven bar) — richer
  cultural cloth presence, but heavier and less legible than the motif at this scale.
- **Subtle atmospheric adire only** — risked still reading as generic premium-dark.

## Consequences

- Adire is demoted to (at most) a faint substrate; it is **not** the load-bearing cultural cue.
- The §7.10 native-speaker reviewer must be told the cultural read rests on the **path motifs +
  Yoruba orthography**, not textile — so orthography correctness (NotoSans, full diacritics)
  becomes even more important.
- Per-path theming is ~3× the main-screen tuning work (one ambient per path), deferred to
  Wave 2; Wave 1 ships the neutral ascent base.
