# Idle-first is the governing design constraint

Every element of the dynasty redesign must pass the check-in test: meaningful in a
~60-second visit, progresses while the player is away, and never demands constant
attention. When the story layer threatened to pull the game toward a
constant-engagement narrative game, we chose to stay idle-first.

This is recorded because the redesign adds narrative systems that *could* drift the
genre, and a future contributor might "improve" engagement by adding exactly the
things this constraint forbids.

## Consequences (binding rules)
- Crossroads density is an idle *rate* (~one per check-in stretch), not denser.
- Crossroads are patient: they queue and wait indefinitely, never timed — a timed
  event punishes absence.
- "Long" comes from the dynasty progressing while away (offline) and from many
  meaningful generations, never from inflating a single life's grind. Active play
  (tap-to-channel) stays strictly optional.
