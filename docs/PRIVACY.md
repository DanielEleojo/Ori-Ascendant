# Privacy Policy — Ori Ascendant

_Last updated: [fill in on publish]_

Ori Ascendant ("the app", "the game") is an offline, single-player idle game. This policy
explains, in plain language, exactly what data the app does and does not handle.

## The short version

**Ori Ascendant does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.** There are no
accounts to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Your game
progress stays on your device and — only if you are signed into Game Center — in your own
private iCloud storage managed by Apple.

## What the app stores

- **Game progress (on your device).** Your cultivation progress, ancestors, lineage bonuses,
  and settings are saved to a private file in the app's own storage. This data describes only
  your in-game state — it contains no name, email, contacts, location, device identifier, or
  any other personal information.
- **Cloud save (optional, via Game Center / iCloud).** If you are signed into Game Center, the
  same game-progress file is synced to your personal iCloud storage so your progress survives
  reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. This sync is handled entirely by Apple's
  Game Center and iCloud. We operate no server and never receive a copy of this data. Your
  Apple ID and its authentication are managed by Apple, not by us.

## What the app does NOT do

- No analytics or telemetry of any kind.
- No advertising or ad networks.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No tracking across apps or websites.
- No collection of personal information (name, email, location, contacts, photos, device IDs).
- No accounts, logins, or sign-ups operated by us.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and is suitable for a
general audience.

## Your control over your data

Your data lives only on your device and in your own iCloud, so you control it entirely.
Deleting the app removes the local save. iCloud / Game Center saved data can be managed or
deleted through your device settings (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage)
and Game Center.

## Third-party services

The app's only external dependency is **Apple Game Center / iCloud**, used solely for the
optional cloud-save sync described above. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's
own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
