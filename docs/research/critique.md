# Critique

## Gaps
- Generation reset scope is undefined: no lens states what resets at gen N+1. Does aseAmount zero out? It must (cumulative thresholds mean carried-over Ase would instantly clear every stage and re-arm the tribulation), but nothing specifies it — nor whether path, the stage-6 escalation state, and the cached rate reset, or what the gen-2 player sees as their starting counter.
- Multi-advance on return is undesigned: with manual Advance + cumulative thresholds, an overnight return in Tier 0/1 banks enough to afford 2-3 stages at once. Does the player tap Advance repeatedly, does it chain, what does the progress bar show when aseAmount exceeds the next threshold (>100% fill?), and is the offline rate always the stage they left at even though they could have advanced? No lens covers the queued-advance UX.
- Path choice flow mechanics are unspecified: what opens the PathScreen (reaching stage index 2? tapping Advance to index 3?), is choosing mandatory to enter Tier 1 or deferrable, is there a confirm step, and is the choice locked for the rest of the generation? Lenses cover card copy and multipliers but not the trigger, blocking behavior, or irreversibility — the player hits this at ~minute 6.
- Tap-to-channel discoverability: MVP has no tutorial beyond tap-to-start, and the mechanic has zero visible affordance until tapped. No lens says how the player learns the portrait is tappable (one-time hint, idle pulse, first-tap callout).
- Ancestor identity generation: prestige-ux shows named ancestors ('Adaeze', 'Ancestor of Radiance/Embers') and the council strip shows icons, but no lens defines where names come from (name pool? culturally vetted list — a cosmology-sensitive asset), or what ancestor art is (cosmology bans egungun imagery and says 'abstract: light/river/flame/stars' but nothing maps ascend-vs-fall or path to specific visuals).
- First-launch screen is undesigned: 'tap-to-start' is named in the exclusions but no lens specifies what that screen contains, or what the first 30 seconds of gen 1 look like (counter at 0, +1.0/s, no instruction — is that the whole opening?).
- Settings and info surfaces are unplaced: the gear icon exists in the layout but its contents are unspecified, and cosmology mandates a glossary/heritage 'About' screen that no lens locates. Likewise nothing says whether tapping the Ancestral Council strip opens a council/shrine detail screen in MVP or is display-only — the gen-2 player will tap it.
- Welcome-Back edge case: a stage-6 overnight banks 54M (>2x the 25M gate) — the flow from Collect straight into a fully-armed 100% tribulation state (escalation skipping 50/80% beats, pulsing button appearing mid-collect) is not sequenced by any lens.

## Conflicts
- Path mechanics: balance and paths lenses ship contradictory designs for all three paths. Balance (load-bearing condition 1): Osun flat x1.5, Sango x1.0 base + x4 surge 30s/180s, Ane x1.25 active / x2 offline. Paths: Osun x1.0 rate but council bonus x2, Sango x2.0 online / x0.5 offline, Ane x1.0 online / x1.5 offline. Every timing table in balance assumes path EV x1.5 on the rate; under the paths-lens design Osun's rate mult is 1.0 (stage-6 grind stretches to ~5h23m for Osun gen-1) and Sango's is 2.0. One design must be picked before any screen copy, HUD badge, or PathConfig field list can be written.
- Path multiplier timing: paths lens claims Sango's Ase/s readout 'visibly doubles the instant of selection' at stage index 2 (its 60-second detectability proof), but the locked design says stages 0-2 are path-less (multiplier 1.0) and balance applies path multipliers only at index >= 3. Sango's headline legibility moment as written contradicts the locked design.
- Tribulation threshold staleness: session-ux specifies the CTA morphs to 'Face Tribulation' at 'stage 6 + 1M Ase' and bases its 2-min check-in pacing on the old 1,000 Tier-1 placeholder ('~17 min idle for stages 0-2'); balance's corrected numbers are 25M tribulation and 100/1,500/5,500 Tier-0 thresholds. All session-ux pacing claims and button-state triggers need re-derivation against the final curve.
- Welcome-Back trigger threshold: session-ux says show the modal only at elapsed >= 180s (silent grant below), unity-impl says >= 60s. Paths lens's Ane detectability script ('lock the phone for 2 minutes, see the itemized x1.5 line') only works under the 60s rule — under 180s, Ane's primary legibility moment never fires in a 2-min lock.
- Gen-2 prestige feel: prestige-ux requires gen 2 to clear stage 1 'under ~30s' so acceleration is viscerally observable; balance's final numbers give 100 Ase / 1.25 per s = 80s. Irreconcilable without changing W (locked at 0.25), BASE_RATE, the stage-1 threshold, or the prestige-ux target.
- Council bonus copy vs Osun: prestige-ux's confirm sheet and balance's UI condition 3 hard-code 'Ascend +25% / Fall +10%' and '+25% Ase from your Ancestor', but paths-lens Osun doubles these to +50%/+20%. The tribulation preview, fall-screen rate delta, and ancestor-card copy must be computed from the active path config, not static strings — and only the paths lens acknowledges this.
- Osun modifies the locked rate formula: councilBonusModifier wrapping (permanentAseBonus + sum of 0.25 x bonusMultiplier) changes the locked formula's shape and makes effective W = 0.5, contradicting balance's 'W = 0.25 (locked)' assumption and silently inflating its long-run council table (full ascended council becomes 3.5x, not 2.25x, hitting balance's 'Tier 2 content needed' line ~3 generations earlier).
- Sango surge vs implementation constraints: balance's Sango (x4 surge for 30s every 180s) requires timer/cooldown state and surge UI, directly contradicting the paths lens's PathConfig design ('exactly 2 double fields, no timers, no new SaveData fields') and session-ux's explicit rejection of timed-boost mechanics as MVP scope creep.
