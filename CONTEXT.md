# Ori Ascendant

Idle cultivation game built on West African cosmology, with a generational
bloodline prestige loop. This glossary fixes the project's shared vocabulary.

## Core resource & self

**Àṣẹ**:
The Yoruba life-force the Cultivator accumulates — the single soft currency and
the fuel for all progression. Always tone-marked Àṣẹ (≠ aṣọ, cloth).
_Avoid_: energy, mana, currency, qi

**Ori**:
The Cultivator's chosen destiny — a single virtue held as a vow (e.g. "the Path of
Mercy"), chosen at birth and measured against at the Crossing. What is tested is
steadfastness to your Ori, not the "rightness" of any choice. The deity-Path
(Ane/Sango/Osun) is a separate facet — how you climb, not what you hold.
_Avoid_: soul, spirit, alignment, morality

**Steadfastness**:
How faithfully a Cultivator's choices hold true to their Ori across a life. The
Crossing is a transparent test of steadfastness — shown to the player, never
hidden — not a flat coin-flip.
_Avoid_: willpower, loyalty, conviction

**Àkùnlẹ̀yàn**:
The choosing of an Ori at the threshold of a life — "that which is chosen
kneeling." Each generation begins by committing to a destiny the life is then
lived to keep. (Spelling subject to §7.10 native-speaker confirmation.)
_Avoid_: spawn, character creation, respawn

## The bloodline

**Cultivator**:
The single hero of one generation — climbs the six Stages, faces one Crossing,
then becomes an Ancestor. Exactly one is active at a time.
_Avoid_: character, hero, unit, player (the player is the Lineage)

**Lineage**:
The bloodline across all generations — the true "player." Persists Àṣẹ bonuses
and the generation count; older Ancestors merge into it over time.
_Avoid_: bloodline, clan, family tree, account

**Ancestor**:
One completed Cultivator (ascended or fallen) seated on the Council. Always an
individual; the merged collective is the Lineage, never "the ancestors."
_Avoid_: hero, spirit, ghost

**Council**:
The up-to-five most recent Ancestors, each lifting the active Cultivator's Àṣẹ
generation. When a sixth joins, the oldest retires into the Lineage.
_Avoid_: party, roster, pantheon

**Lineage blessing**:
The single displayed number for the Council's combined uplift to Àṣẹ generation.
_Avoid_: council bonus, council factor

## Progression

**Stage**:
One of six cultivation tiers a Cultivator climbs each generation (0–5 internally;
shown as Stage 1–6). In order: Ọmọ Ayé, Akẹ́kọ̀ọ́, Awo, Aláàṣẹ, Àgbà, Aṣẹ́gun.
_Avoid_: realm, level, rank

**Tier**:
The two halves of the Stage ladder — Ayé (Stages 1–3) and Ọ̀run (Stages 4–6).
The Path is chosen at the Ayé→Ọ̀run boundary.
_Avoid_: act, chapter, phase

**The Crossing (Ìrékọjá)**:
The once-per-generation capstone where the Cultivator ascends or falls, producing
an Ancestor and resetting to Stage 1. "Tribulation" is the accepted xianxia-register
synonym for the same event.
_Avoid_: prestige, rebirth, reincarnation

**Ascend / Ascended**:
A successful Crossing — a full-strength Ancestor; shown as gold radiance.
_Avoid_: win, succeed, level-up

**Fall / Fallen**:
An unsuccessful Crossing — still a (lesser) Ancestor, honoured, never punished;
warm embers, never grey/red/death.
_Avoid_: death, defeat, fail, loss, game over

## Paths

**Path**:
The tradition a Cultivator commits to for a generation — Ane, Sango, or Osun —
re-chosen each generation. The Path is the mechanic; its emblem is the "path
motif" and its cosmetic trim the "path accent" (art terms, not synonyms).
_Avoid_: class, element, discipline

**Ane**:
The Igala earth Path — endurance; rewards offline progress. (Igala — distinct
from Igbo Ala / Yoruba Ilẹ̀.)

**Sango**:
The Yoruba thunder Path — sudden force; rewards active play. Patron via documented
attributes (lightning), never a figure or the double-axe.

**Osun**:
The Yoruba river Path — flow; rewards the Council. Patron via documented
attributes (flowing water), never a woman/mermaid.

## Presentation

**Appearance**:
The cosmetic look a Cultivator is rendered with for one generation, drawn from a
small fixed **appearance pool** and rolled at the Crossing — purely visual,
independent of Stage, Path, and all rates. The pool entries are Appearances, not
Cultivators.
_Avoid_: character, roster, skin, "the 3 cultivators"

**Ascended reveal**:
The brief transfiguration shown when a Cultivator ascends at the Crossing — the
same person made exalted: crowned and robed in radiance, dignity carried by
bearing and regalia, never a remade or idealised physique. Seen for a beat, then
the Cultivator passes into the abstract Lineage.
_Avoid_: power form, god form, prime form

## The dynasty story

**Crossroads**:
A choice the player meets along the climb — drawn from an authored, culture-vetted
deck and queued so it waits patiently for the next check-in (never timed). The
choice tests steadfastness to the Ori and is inked into the Chronicle. A player
authors the line's story by the crossroads they take.
_Avoid_: event, encounter, quest, card

**Chronicle**:
The accreting, per-player record of the Lineage — each Cultivator's Ori, deeds, and
how the Crossing remembered them. The dynasty's story, assembled from vetted
authored parts; unique to each player by the choices and outcomes that fill it.
_Avoid_: log, history, journal, save file

**Deed**:
A consequential choice, recorded — the unit the Chronicle is built from, and the
material an ascended Title or a fallen Nickname is drawn from.
_Avoid_: action, achievement, quest

**Title**:
How an *ascended* Cultivator is remembered in the Chronicle — their final Stage
name borne as an honorific, plus a personal name (e.g. "Aṣẹ́gun Adé").
_Avoid_: realm title, rank

**Nickname**:
How a *fallen* Cultivator is remembered in the Chronicle — an epithet drawn from
how their life went, with no Stage title (e.g. "the one who turned back at the ford").
_Avoid_: title (a Nickname is precisely the absence of one)
